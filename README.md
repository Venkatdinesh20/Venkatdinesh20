## 👋 About Me

I'm **VenkatDinesh Pasupuleti**, a **Senior AI/ML Engineer** with **8+ years** of experience designing and shipping production-grade AI systems — from agentic LLM pipelines to fraud-detection models running at scale. I care about building things that are defensible under questioning: grounded, evaluated, monitored, and audit-ready, not just demo-ready.

- 🔭 Currently building **agentic AI systems** with LangGraph — orchestrator + specialist agents, guardrails, and human-in-the-loop escalation
- 🧠 Deep focus on **RAG & retrieval** — hybrid BM25 + FAISS, reranking, citation enforcement, and refusal-over-hallucination design
- 📊 8 years turning **ML models into regulated, production systems** — fraud/credit-risk scoring with full audit trails and explainability
- 🎥 I create content on **YouTube, Medium, and Instagram**, breaking down AI/ML engineering for practitioners
- ☕ Fun fact: my best architecture decisions happen somewhere between the second cup of coffee and the first failed unit test
